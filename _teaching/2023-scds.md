Flipped-classroom format course, co-taught with Prof. [Thomas Schrøder](https://forskning.ruc.dk/da/persons/tbs) focused on giving an overview of major topics in scientific computing and data science. In-class activities followed Jupyter notebooks hosted [here](https://gitlab.com/jasnyder/scientific-computing-and-data-science-f2023). Topics in the data science portion of the course follow the book [*Data-Driven Science and Engineering*](https://databookuw.com/) by Steve Brunton and J. Nathan Kutz, and make use of recorded lectures from Steve Brunton's [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@Eigensteve)

Topics include:
* Scientific Computing
    * Programming in Python; numpy, scipy, data visualization
    * Numerical integration and differentiation
    * Sovling IVPs
    * Solving PDEs
    * Molecular dynamics
    * Optimization
* Data Science
    * Regression and Model selection
    * Clustering and Classification
    * Neural networks
    * Data-driven dynamical systems (SINDy)
    * Singular value decomposition
